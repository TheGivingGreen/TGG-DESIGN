Sponsorship tier card. Always on forest, always with a 6px accent rail at the top.

```jsx
<TierCard accent="flag" label="Official Category Partner" price="$10,000" availability="2 Available"
  benefits={['Two sponsored holes, your signature drink at each', 'Back-of-scorecard logo beside the Title Partner']} />
```

Rail colour descends by tier: Flag, Bunker, Fairway. Benefits use gold interpuncts, not checkmarks.
