Ticket tier card for the tickets grid.

```jsx
<TicketCard featured badge="The Ticket" badgeTone="forest" name="Team Shirt Entry" price="$45"
  priceNote="per player"
  includes={['Your team shirt, which is your event pass', '$15 to the charity you choose at checkout', 'Physical scorecard for all nine holes']}
  cta="Get on the Tee Sheet" ctaVariant="primary" ctaHref="https://thegivinggreen.store" />
```

The shirt is the ticket, so the shirt tier is always the featured one. Price is Anton in Flag red.
