Duotones every marketing photo forest-over-sand, and optionally scrims it for type.

```jsx
<DuotoneImage src="assets/images/photos/hero.webp" alt="Year One crowd" ratio="16/9" scrim opacity={0.55}>
  <h1 style={{position:'relative'}}>THE 2026 MAIN EVENT</h1>
</DuotoneImage>
```

- Editorial strips: `opacity={1}`, no scrim, hairline 3px gaps between figures.
- Behind headlines: `opacity` .40 to .55 plus `scrim`.
- Product photography in the Pro Shop stays full colour. Do not duotone hats or shirts.
