Pill button for every action in the system; `primary` is reserved for the ticket CTA.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" href="https://thegivinggreen.store">Get on the Tee Sheet</Button>
<Button variant="ghost">Meet the Charities</Button>
```

- `primary` is Flag red. One per viewport, and it should say buy a ticket.
- `ghost` on sand, `ghostOnDark` on forest. Both fill solid on hover.
- `forest` is the quiet solid, used for "Join This Team" inside cards; it hovers to Fairway.
- Sizes: sm 12px / md 13px / lg 14px. Never below sm.
