Standard three-part section opener. Use it for every section so the page keeps its rhythm.

```jsx
<SectionHead eyebrow="02 · How to Play" title={<>Compete Against<br/>Your Friends.</>}
  subline="Just like real golf, the lowest score wins." />
```

- Line-break the headline yourself with `<br/>`; Anton headlines are composed, not wrapped.
- `onDark` flips the headline to cream and the subline to Bunker gold.
- Center only for short mid-page interludes (scoring, scorecard features).
